01 / Perspective

Connecting hospital realities
with technology delivery.

A healthcare technology professional focused on the systems, people and processes that make software work in real clinical environments.

My work spans healthcare SaaS operations, HIMS implementation, quality assurance, user training, release coordination and customer-facing technical delivery.

I collaborate across hospital teams, support functions, product and engineering to understand operational problems, structure requirements, coordinate resolution and support adoption. I am particularly interested in improving the full journey — from workflow discovery and UAT through training, go-live and post-launch stabilization.

My approach combines hands-on troubleshooting with process thinking: clarify the issue, identify the root cause, align stakeholders, document the decision and make the improvement repeatable.
